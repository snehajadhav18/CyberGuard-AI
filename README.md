# CyberGuard AI

## AI-Powered Cybersecurity Awareness & Phishing Risk Analysis

CyberGuard AI is a cybersecurity awareness platform designed to help users identify and understand potential online threats such as phishing, social engineering, suspicious links, impersonation attempts, and credential theft.

Instead of only looking for obvious scam keywords, CyberGuard AI analyzes the overall context of a message and provides an explainable risk assessment.

## Key Features

### 🔍 Message Analyzer
Analyzes emails, SMS messages, and other text for potential cybersecurity threats.

It evaluates signals such as:
- Social engineering
- Urgency and pressure
- Account or security threats
- Credential theft attempts
- Financial fraud indicators
- Impersonation
- Suspicious URLs
- Domain and trust-signal characteristics

The analyzer provides:
- Overall risk score
- Risk level
- Security signal breakdown
- Explanation of why the message was flagged
- Recommended safety actions

### 🔐 Password Advisor
Provides guidance for creating stronger and safer passwords and helps users understand common password-security practices.

### 🤖 Cyber Chatbot
Provides beginner-friendly cybersecurity awareness guidance and answers common security-related questions.

### 🛡️ Safety Tips
Provides practical cybersecurity recommendations for safer online behavior.

## What Makes CyberGuard AI Different?

Many basic phishing detectors focus mainly on obvious keywords such as "urgent", "winner", or "click here".

CyberGuard AI focuses on **context and multiple security signals**. A message may appear professional and legitimate while still attempting to create urgency, impersonate a trusted service, or redirect the user to a suspicious website.

The platform combines these indicators into an explainable risk assessment so that users can understand not only whether a message is risky, but also **why** it is risky.

## Project Goal

The goal of CyberGuard AI is to make cybersecurity awareness more accessible to beginners by converting complex security concepts into simple, understandable risk assessments and recommendations.

## Disclaimer

CyberGuard AI is an awareness and educational tool. Its results should not be treated as a guarantee that a message, link, or communication is completely safe.

Users should avoid sharing passwords, OTPs, banking information, or other sensitive information when a communication appears suspicious.
